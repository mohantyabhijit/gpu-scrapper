# Raster visual direction

**Impeccable seed:** `user-pinned-canon-stripe-craft-2026-08-23`

The user pinned a familiar, Stripe-quality minimalist direction. This is the
standing canon path, so no randomized concept seed displaced it. Stripe is the
craft benchmark, never a source of copied brand assets or composition.

## 1. World

An original light enterprise data product: warm-white ground, ink and slate
type, fine cool-gray rules, restrained cobalt/cyan actions, small green health
signals, 10-14px corner language, and soft offset depth only where hierarchy
needs it. The world is calm, exact, and evidence-led.

## 2. First viewport

The visitor sees the product mechanism immediately: market-consistent public
retailers flow through Scraper Studio, schema validation, and into one
market-local offer view. The market controls and opening comparison evidence
must begin inside a standard desktop viewport; mobile must show all four trace
stages without requiring horizontal scrolling.

## 3. Visitor path

Understand the trace, inspect fixture/live disclosure, choose a market, compare
offers in native currency, then verify at the attributed retailer. Data health
is the operator/judge continuation and uses the same visual grammar.

## 4. Signature interaction

The source trace changes its retailer, market, currency, and evidence wording
with the selected market. Market selection submits immediately through a
URL-owned GET, while every later filter remains inspectable and shareable.

## 5. Cross-surface reach and risk

The light ledger system governs the homepage, Data health, method, and detail
routes. The honest risk is over-compressing complex evidence into decorative
cards; list and table structures must remain legible, accessible, and truthful.

## Quality bar

- The first viewport demonstrates collection and validation rather than merely
  describing them.
- Every trace stage uses one authored outline SVG family and market-consistent
  facts.
- No cross-market ranking, commerce language, invented live proof, gradient
  text, glass decoration, italic serif display, or generic icon-card scaffold.
- Body and supporting text meet WCAG AA; dense ledger copy is at least 11px and
  remains readable at mobile zoom.
- Desktop and mobile captures are compared to the approved comp before ship.
