# Design direction

HackRadar is a working desk for repeat builders, not a generic events directory. The visual rhythm alternates an editorial hero, compact market metrics, and ledger-like ranked rows. Fine typography and micro-interactions create polish while the information architecture remains direct.

Each market gets an accent palette; content density and interaction locations stay stable to preserve learned behavior. Three.js responds to pointer movement and degrades gracefully when WebGL or animation is unavailable.
