# HackRadar product brief

HackRadar is a decision surface for people who join many hackathons. Its job is to answer: “What is worth building next from my country?”

The product provides a deduplicated worldwide top ten plus eligibility-aware views for the USA, India, UK, and Singapore. Rankings use disclosed USD-equivalent prize values, with unknown/non-cash awards last. Country views also show estimated INR, GBP, or SGD equivalents alongside USD; the US and worldwide views use USD as their comparison currency. AI, Web3, Web, Mobile, Climate, and Other filters reduce the field. Every entry shows the original source, schedule, deadline, participation mode, source prize claim, and a Weekend/Focused/Marathon effort estimate.

The organizer page is authoritative. HackRadar does not run events, accept applications, guarantee awards, or make legal eligibility determinations.

Success means a frequent builder can change country, understand the strongest opportunities, assess effort, and reach the original page in under a minute. It also means a source can drift without erasing the last usable data or forcing frontend schema changes.
